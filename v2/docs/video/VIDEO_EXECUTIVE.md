# Executive brief — Can you trust the plan?

> **NotebookLM instruction (paste into Video Overview → Customize):**
> Audience: executives, sponsors, and steering-committee members of regulated

> product demo. Length: STRICTLY 2–3 minutes. Follow the narration sections in
> order; do not add product detail beyond what is written here.

**Goal:** an executive understands in under 3 minutes why status from this
system is more trustworthy than a normal SteerCo pack — and what to ask for.

---

## Scene 1 — The question (0:00–0:30)

Every steering committee reads a green dashboard and privately asks the same
question: is this actually true, or is it what the project wants us to see?
Traditional status packs can't answer that — every number in them was typed by
someone with an interest in how it looks.

## Scene 2 — Computed, not claimed (0:30–1:15)

In this command center, the headline confidence score cannot be typed. It is
computed from the project's own records — cost efficiency, schedule pace, and
forecast — and if the data is too thin to judge, it says so rather than
inventing a verdict. A brand-new plan reads "Plan only", because a plan that
hasn't started hasn't earned trust.

When claimed progress disagrees with what the records show, the report says
that too, in writing. A pack that flags its own weak spots is a pack you can
believe.

## Scene 3 — Slips are decisions, not surprises (1:15–2:00)

The go-live date your programme committed to is frozen. If it moves, the
record shows who moved it, when, and why. When a schedule slip is absorbed,
it's absorbed as a named, logged decision that appears in your next brief —
not discovered three months later. Every number in the brief traces to source
records in one click: the answer to "says who?" is built in.

## Scene 4 — The ask (2:00–2:30)

Your data stays on your side: records live locally, not on a vendor's cloud —
which your compliance team will appreciate. The ask is simple: have one
troubled project run through it for one reporting cycle, and compare the brief
you get against the one you got last month. The difference is the point.
